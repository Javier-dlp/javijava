# CV source

Edit the CV together in the [canonical Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Qwbjo0rdLkpmYcVpHaJtIoVlxx2webBXl_KDUHVjVJE/edit).

After accepting changes, export it as PDF and replace `Javier_de_la_Pena_CV.pdf` in this folder. Check the exported PDF's layout before publishing.

The Google Doc is the editable source of truth; the PDF is the website and application download. Do not maintain separate Word, Pages, or OpenOffice copies.
